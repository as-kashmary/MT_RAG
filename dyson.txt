To start using your Dyson V11 Cord-Free Vacuum Cleaner, remove it from the box and attach the
wand to the main body by aligning the connections and pressing until you hear a click. Insert your
preferred cleaning head, such as the High Torque cleaner head, into the wand and ensure it is firmly
attached. Power on the vacuum by pressing the power button on the handle. The Dyson V11
features three cleaning modes: Eco, Auto, and Boost. Select the mode that best suits your cleaning
needs by pressing the mode button on the handle. Once powered on, the vacuum will automatically
adjust the suction power based on your cleaning mode. After use, store the vacuum on its charging
dock to ensure it's always ready for the next cleaning session. The charging dock ensures that your
vacuum remains powered for continuous use.

Troubleshooting:
If your Dyson V11 Cord-Free Vacuum Cleaner is not turning on, ensure the battery is fully charged by
checking the LED status light on the battery. If the battery is empty, place the vacuum on the charging
dock and allow it to charge for a minimum of 4.5 hours. If the vacuum still does not work after
charging, check that the main body and attachments are correctly connected. If the motorhead or
wand is blocked, check for any debris or obstruction and remove it. If the vacuum is losing suction,
ensure the dustbin is empty and that the filter is clean. Regularly check for any blockages in the hose
or brush bar, as debris can affect the suction power. If the vacuum continues to have issues, reset the
device by pressing and holding the power button for 10 seconds to restart it.
